What we do here

Below are the main treatments at K-Glow. Each one has a job. Each one fits certain skin. Your provider will help you pick the right mix for your goal.

Botox

Botox can soften lines from frowns, squints, and smiles. It works best on the forehead, between the brows, and at the eye corners. The shots are small and fast. The pinch is light. Most people go back to their day right after. An Aesthetics Office Lubbock Texas Botox visit often starts with a light touch. The soft change shows in a few days and grows for about two weeks.

Dermal Fillers

Fillers can add soft shape where the face has lost it. They can help cheeks look full. They can help lips look smooth. They can help lines by the mouth look less deep. The gel goes in with a fine needle. You see the shape at once in the mirror. A little swelling for a day is normal.

XERF

XERF uses warm energy under the skin. It helps skin feel firm over time. No cut is made. The visit is calm. The change builds over weeks. It can help the face and neck feel more tight. Ask if it fits your skin type.

LaseMD Ultra

LaseMD Ultra is a light treatment for tone and texture. It can help sun spots fade. It can help rough skin feel smooth. It can help skin look bright. The skin may be pink for a day. After that, it often looks fresh. Sun care after is key.

Potenza RF Microneedling

Potenza uses small needles and heat. The needles make tiny spots in the skin. The heat helps skin build collagen. That can help lines, acne scars, and big pores. It can help skin that feels loose. Red skin for a day or two is normal. The firm feel grows over weeks.

PicoSure Pro

PicoSure Pro is a laser with fast light pulses. It can break up dark spots and old acne marks. It can help tone look even. It can fade some tattoos. The visit is short. You may feel a light snap. Your provider will say how many visits may fit your goal.

Keralase

Keralase helps hair and scalp health. A laser makes small paths on the scalp. A serum is placed on those paths. The aim is hair that looks fuller with time. It takes a set of visits. Your provider will check your scalp first and talk about what to expect.

Titanium Laser Hair Removal

This laser aims at hair at the root to slow growth. It can be used on legs, arms, underarms, and the bikini line. It can be used on the face for some people. Hair grows in rounds, so a set of visits is needed. Shave before you come in. Skip waxing and plucking before it.

Skin Boosters

Skin Boosters are small shots that help skin hold water. They can make dry skin look soft. They can make fine lines look less sharp. They do not add big volume. They add a light glow. They are often used on the face, neck, and hands. Small bumps after can fade in a day.

Questions about aesthetics office visits

Can I bring someone with me?

Yes. You can bring a friend or a family member to your consult. They can sit with you and help you recall the plan. They can ask questions, too.

Do I need a ride home?

Most people drive home on their own. Most treatments do not make you feel drowsy. If you feel sore, rest in the office for a bit before you leave.

What if I am new to skin care?

That is fine. Many people are new. Your provider will start with the basics. A soft wash, a plain cream, and sun care each day can go a long way.

How often should I come back?

It depends on your plan. Some people come back in weeks. Some come back in months. Your provider will give you a time that fits your treatment and your goal.

Can I get two treatments on one day?

Yes, in many cases. Some pairs work well together. Some need to be on different days. Your provider will tell you the safe order for your skin.

What should I skip before a visit?

Skip strong scrubs and strong acids for a few days before some treatments. Skip sun the day before a laser visit if you can. Your provider will give you a short list when you book.

How do I pay for my visit?

You will hear the cost before you start. Ask about the price for your plan when you call (806) 805-1530. There are no hidden fees on the day of your visit.
